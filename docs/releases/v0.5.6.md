## 更新内容

- 完整翻唱可以拖动波形选段，重做后在 A 和 B 之间同步试听，确认后再采用 B 版。
- 使用统计默认关闭。愿意分享生成、首次导出和采用 B 版次数的用户，可以在安装文件夹中运行 `Analytics Preferences.cmd` 开启或关闭。统计只包含粗略参数和已登记公开曲目的编号，音频与输入文字留在本机。

## Updates

- Select a region on a completed cover, redo it, and compare A and B at the same playback position before choosing a version.
- Usage statistics are off by default. Use `Analytics Preferences.cmd` in the installation folder to opt in or opt out. Reports count generation, first export clicks, and B-version adoption. They contain coarse settings and IDs of cataloged public songs. Audio and text remain on the device.
