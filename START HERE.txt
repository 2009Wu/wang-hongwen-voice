WANG HONGWEN VOICE STUDIO - WINDOWS

1. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder where you want to keep the app.
2. Double-click "Start Voice Studio.exe" inside that folder.
3. Click "Set up & open Voice Studio" and wait for the setup to finish.

The app opens in your web browser automatically. Later, open the same EXE
and click "Open Voice Studio". Keep the launcher open while using the app.

You need:
- 64-bit Windows 10 or 11
- An NVIDIA graphics card with 16 GB or more video memory
- A current NVIDIA graphics driver
- At least 30 GB of free space on the app's drive
- Internet access for the first setup and first model downloads

Python, the GPU software libraries, FFmpeg, and the app environments are
set up automatically inside .runtime. AI models are kept in .cache.
First setup downloads several GB and can take a while. No Docker,
terminal commands, manual Python setup, or account login is needed.

If setup is interrupted, open the launcher again and choose Repair setup.
It reuses completed downloads and installed packages. If you move the app
folder after setup, run Repair setup at its new location.

Close the browser tab whenever you like. Click Stop or close the launcher
to shut down the local app. Save your generated audio before closing it.

More help: docs/Windows-Setup.md
Project: https://github.com/2009Wu/wang-hongwen-voice

All generated speech and singing are AI interpretations, not authentic
recordings or performances.
